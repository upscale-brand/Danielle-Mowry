# Danielle Mowry site content notes

- Site content is adapted from the public website https://www.daniellemowrynutrition.com/ and publicly indexed LinkedIn material. Full LinkedIn profile access was restricted during research.
- Danielle Mowry, MS, RDN, CSOWM, CLC, LDN; founder of Danielle Mowry Nutrition, LLC, and The Maternal Nutrition Lab.
- Focus: evidence-based maternal and lactation nutrition education for healthcare professionals across pregnancy, postpartum, and breastfeeding.
- Courses: Easy Prenatal Nutrition; Breastfeeding Nutrition Made Simple; Postnatal Nutrition for Healing & Energy. The website lists the complete bundle at 11.25 CEUs. Individual page and homepage CEU figures are not fully consistent, so the site avoids individual course credits.
- Free guide: “10 Maternal Nutrition Counseling Pearls Every Healthcare Professional Should Know,” with counseling pearls, ready-to-use clinical language, and quick-reference tables.
- Site palette is a visual approximation from the live branding: white/off-white #FDFCF8, charcoal #25251F, rose #C44778, and sage #718A50. Exact source color values were not exposed in the website render.
- The client-supplied Danielle Mowry Nutrition PNG is used as the wordmark.
- Template structure, layout, palette switcher, navigation, and responsive behaviors are retained. Scroll and hover motion is lightweight and honors reduced-motion preferences.
- Legal pages remain template drafts for review before launch.
